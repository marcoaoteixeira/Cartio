Cartio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026 · Publisher: Minicore Software

Cartio is a shopping list and expense tracking app. This page explains what data the app handles and why. It is written to match exactly what the current version of the app does — not a generic template.

No account, no sign-in

Cartio has no login or account system. You never give us a name, email, or any identity to use the app.

Your shopping lists and expenses stay on your device

Every shopping list, item, and expense record you create is stored locally on your device, in a private database the app manages. Minicore Software does not operate a server, and nothing you type into Cartio is sent to us.

Android device backup

Cartio allows Android's built-in Auto Backup feature, which can include the app's local data in your own Android device backup to your Google Account. This is controlled entirely by Google and your device settings, not by Minicore Software — we have no access to it. You can disable this at any time in your Android system settings.

Advertising

Cartio shows ads using Google's AdMob (part of the Google Mobile Ads SDK) to support free use of the app. To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect device and advertising identifiers and usage data, under Google's own privacy policy. Minicore Software does not receive or store this data directly. You can see and manage your ad personalization settings in your device's Google settings, and learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

In-app purchase

Cartio offers an optional one-time "Buy Me A Coffee" purchase to remove ads, processed entirely through Google Play's billing system. Minicore Software never sees your payment details — Google Play reports back only whether the purchase was completed.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools are included in the app.
  • We do not sell or share your data, because we do not collect any to begin with.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Children's privacy

Cartio is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Cartio changes how data is handled (for example, an optional cloud sync feature), this page will be updated to reflect it before that feature ships, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
